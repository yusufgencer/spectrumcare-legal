2. Whose data we process

SpectrumCare involves two groups of people:

  • You, the user — the caregiver who owns the account.
  • The person you care for — the subject of the records, in most cases a child.

If the care recipient is a child or an adult unable to give their own consent, by using the app you confirm that you are authorised to act on their behalf as a parent or legal guardian. The explicit consent you give covers your own data on your own behalf, and the care recipient's data on theirs.

4. How voice notes are processed

We describe this separately because your audio leaves your device:

  1. When you start recording, audio is held temporarily on your device.
  2. The audio is sent to an AI provider via OpenRouter to be transcribed into text.
  3. The returned text and the draft records derived from it are saved to your account.
  4. Raw audio is not stored on our servers — only the text is kept.

So while your audio file is not retained, it is transmitted to a third party for processing. If you do not want health-related speech sent to an AI provider, do not use the voice note feature — every record can also be entered manually.

AI output is a draft and is never saved as a record until you approve it. The app does not produce diagnoses, treatments, or medication recommendations.

6. International transfers

Our database servers are located in the European Union. AI providers may be located outside the European Union, for example in the United States. By using the voice note and summary features you also consent to the relevant content being transferred to those countries.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Your records are kept until you delete them or close your account.
  • Raw audio recordings are not retained.
  • When you delete your account, your care records and the care recipient's data are deleted. Records we are legally required to keep (such as invoices and payment records) are retained for the period required by law.

8. Withdrawing your consent

You may withdraw your explicit consent to the processing of health data at any time, in the app under Account → Privacy and security, or by writing to us. After withdrawal no new health data is processed, and existing records are deleted on your request. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out lawfully before that point.

9. Your rights

Under GDPR and Turkish KVKK Article 11 you have the right to access your data, request its correction or deletion, request restriction of processing, receive your data in a portable format, and object to processing. Send requests to [contact email]; we respond within 30 days at the latest.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority, or with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by row-level security rules: only caregivers invited to a given care recipient can reach that person's records. No system is perfectly secure; in the event of a breach we will notify you and the competent authority within the period required by law.

11. Children's data

The app is intended for use by caregivers and is not directed at children as users. Where the care recipient is a child, their data is processed with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. Children's data is never used for advertising or profiling.
